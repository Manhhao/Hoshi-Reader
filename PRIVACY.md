# Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device unless you use the features below.

**Google Drive Sync** - If enabled, your books, reading progress, statistics, highlights, shelves, book covers and Sasayaki data can be uploaded to your Google Drive. The app can only access files that were created by the app and cannot read any other files in your Drive. Sign-in is handled through Google directly, the app only stores authentication tokens.

**Audio** - When you play word audio or have Anki audio export configured, the word and reading are sent to a Cloudflare Worker by default. The default audio source can be disabled, and additional user-configurable audio sources can be added.

**Anki** - When you export a word to AnkiMobile, user-configurable word data is shared via URL schemes. If AnkiConnect is enabled, it is sent to the configured server URL instead. If you log in to an Anki sync server, your username and password are sent to that server, your collection is downloaded to check for duplicates and media can be uploaded to it. No password is stored, only your username and a key returned by the server are kept on the device.

**Third-Party Services** - Google Drive, Cloudflare and a configured Anki sync server are used as described above. These services are governed by their respective privacy policies.

**Data Deletion** - All local data is deleted when you uninstall the app. Google Drive data can be deleted directly from your Google Drive. You can sign out of Google Drive or the Anki sync server at any time to remove stored authentication tokens.

No analytics, tracking, or advertising is used.

For questions, open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/Manhhao/Hoshi-Reader/issues).
